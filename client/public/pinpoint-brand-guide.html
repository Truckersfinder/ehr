12 — Motion

Motion & Animation

Motion communicates, it doesn't decorate. Every animation has a purpose. Prefer ease-out for elements entering the screen; ease-in for exits. Clinical data should never animate distractingly.

Page Load / Reveal
Staggered fade-up on section load. 60ms delay between child elements. Used for hero, feature cards, and section content.
fadeUp 0.6s ease · delay: i×80ms
Hover State
Cards lift 3–4px with shadow increase on hover. Buttons translate -1–2px. All hover transitions: 0.2–0.25s ease.
transform: translateY(-3px) · 0.2s ease
Floating Elements
Decorative floating cards use a slow sinusoidal vertical loop. 3s duration, 1.5s offset between pairs. Pure CSS.
floatY 3s ease-in-out infinite
Status Pulse
Live/active badge dots pulse at 2s intervals using scale + opacity. Signals real-time sync or online state.
pulse 2s infinite · scale(1.4) opacity(0.5)
Scroll Reveal
Sections fade up as they enter viewport via IntersectionObserver. Threshold: 10% visibility. One-shot, not repeating.
opacity: 0→1 · translateY(20px→0) · 0.5s
Navigation Focus
Nav border-left accent, color, and background all transition at 0.15s. Fast — inputs and nav must feel instant.
border-left + bg + color · 0.15s ease

13 — Voice

Voice & Tone

Imani speaks with authority and warmth. We are clinically precise without being cold. We celebrate Africa without othering it. We are a tool, not a personality.

🎯 Precise Clear, specific, and accurate. We never use vague language for clinical or technical claims.
🤝 Grounded Built in Africa, for Africa. We speak to the realities of our users, not aspirational fictions.
🏛️ Trustworthy Clinical tools carry lives. Our language projects confidence without arrogance.
✨ Refined Elegant, not verbose. We say more with less. No filler, no buzzwords.
✓
Do Write This
"247 patient records synced successfully across 3 facilities."
"Your NHIA claim was approved. Payment expected in 3–5 business days."
"Lab results available. Review Fatima Koné's CBC before her 10:30 appointment."
"Offline mode active. All changes will sync when connection is restored."
"Built for the African healthcare ecosystem — from Lagos to Nairobi."
✕
Don't Write This
"Leveraging cutting-edge AI to revolutionize African healthcare."
"Your claim may or may not have been submitted. Please try again later."
"We're so excited to announce an awesome new feature!"
"Error 504. Something went wrong. Please contact your administrator."
"Bringing world-class solutions to the developing world."
14 — Logo Usage

Do & Don't

These rules protect the integrity of the Imani brand. When in doubt, use an approved file from the asset library and consult the design team.

✓
Do
Use the logo on white, cream, or deep green backgrounds only.
Maintain the minimum exclusion zone on all four sides at all times.
Use approved SVG or PNG files — never recreate the mark manually.
Use the wordmark and mark together unless in a context smaller than 24px height.
Use the reversed (white) version on any dark or photographic background.
Scale the logo proportionally only — never stretch or squish.
✕
Don't
Place the logo on busy photographs, patterned backgrounds, or non-brand colors.
Rotate, skew, or apply drop shadows to the logo.
Change the color of the wordmark or mark individually.
Use the terracotta or gold backgrounds for the primary wordmark version.
Combine the Imani mark with another brand's logo at equal visual weight.
Use the logo at sizes below 80px wide — use the mark-only version instead.

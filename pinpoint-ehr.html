Platform Features

Everything your facility needs,
nothing it doesn't

Purpose-built for African healthcare realities — from community clinics to multi-site hospital networks.

Smart Patient Records

Comprehensive electronic health records with support for local disease coding, indigenous treatment notes, and multi-provider access across your facility network.

Offline-First Architecture

Designed for intermittent connectivity. All clinical workflows function without internet and sync automatically when connection is restored — zero data loss.

Multi-Language Support

Interface and clinical templates available in English, French, Swahili, Hausa, Yoruba, Amharic, and more — meeting your clinical team where they are.

NHIS & Insurance Integration

Pre-built connectors for NHIA (Ghana), NHIS (Nigeria), NHIF (Kenya), and other national health insurance schemes — streamlining claims and approvals.

Pharmacy & Formulary

Integrated medication management with WHO Essential Medicines list, local drug availability checks, dosing alerts, and prescription tracking.

Public Health Analytics

Disease surveillance dashboards, outbreak detection, maternal health tracking, and automated DHIS2 reporting for ministry compliance.

Lab & Diagnostics

Order management, result tracking, and reference range support calibrated for African lab standards — including rapid diagnostic test workflows.

Mobile-First Design

Full clinical functionality on low-end Android devices and feature phones via USSD — built for the hardware realities of African health workers.

Security & Compliance

HIPAA, NDPR (Nigeria), POPIA (South Africa), and DPA (Ghana) compliant with role-based access, audit trails, and local data residency options.
